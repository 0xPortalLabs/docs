Settlement brings each points market to completion, allowing users to claim their share of tokens and any funds from missed settlements.

The settlement type determines whether allocations are delivered by sellers or through points escrow.

| Settlement type | Points access | Allocation delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller settlement | Read-only | Sellers deliver their allocations. |
| Oracle escrow settlement | Read access and points transfers | Allocation delivered through escrow. |

<Info>
The settlement type also determines the project fee share. See [Fees & Revenue](/partners/fees-and-revenue) for the fee structure.
</Info>

## Seller settlement

Checkpoint verifies points balances through the adapter. Sold points remain in the seller's account within the project's points system.

When settlement becomes available, sellers must deliver the required allocation within the settlement window. Overdue offers enter the market's default process.

This is the standard settlement type and does not require project participation to open a market.

## Oracle escrow settlement

The adapter provides read access to points balances. Oracle escrow settlement also requires a method to transfer sold points from users' accounts to the designated oracle address within the project's points system. General backend write access is not required.

Oracle escrow markets do not require seller collateral, reducing upfront capital requirements and simplifying the user experience.

The project must preserve allocation eligibility for escrowed points and make the resulting allocation available to the settlement contracts. See [Project Commitments](/partners/commitments) for the detailed obligations.

This allows the allocation to be delivered without requiring each seller to settle individually. The arrangement must be established before the market opens.

<Note>
Moving points into escrow does not complete settlement. The associated allocation must still become available and reach the settlement contracts.
</Note>

## Claims and fee distribution

### User claims

Once the program’s distribution is finalized, users can claim their share of the collected tokens and any funds from missed settlements. Claims are based on their escrow token holdings.

See [Program Settlement](/architecture/program-settlement) for the claim process.

### Project fee claims

Eligible projects can claim their fee share after markets settle, under the applicable conditions and schedule. A signed [standard agreement](/partners/commitments) and fulfillment of the applicable TGE and allocation-delivery commitments are required to receive fees.

The fee split is fixed on market open and cannot be changed afterward.
