Markets can use seller settlement or an oracle integration. Oracle integrations cover allocation-based settlement or ongoing redemption, depending on the points program.

| Market model | Points access | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller settlement | Read-only | Sellers deliver their allocations. |
| Oracle escrow settlement | Read access and points transfers | Allocation delivered through escrow. |
| Ongoing redemption | Read and write access | Redemption flow developed with the project. |

<Info>
The integration type also determines the project fee share. See [Project Fees](/partners/fees-and-revenue) for the fee structure.
</Info>

## Market models

The market model determines how sold points are handled and how buyers receive token allocations or redeem points for benefits.

### Seller settlement

Checkpoint verifies points balances through the adapter. Sold points remain in the seller’s account and sellers are responsible for delivering the allocation for their filled offers.

Buyer payments remain locked until the seller settles. Sellers must deliver the required allocation within the settlement window to receive payment. If they miss the deadline, the held payment and any posted collateral enter the program-wide distribution pool.

This is the standard model and does not require project participation to open a market.

### Oracle escrow settlement

The adapter provides read access to points balances. Oracle escrow settlement also requires a method to transfer sold points from users' accounts to the designated oracle address within the project's points system. General backend write access is not required.

Oracle escrow markets do not require seller collateral, reducing upfront capital requirements and simplifying the user experience.

The project must preserve allocation eligibility for escrowed points and make the resulting allocation available to the settlement contracts. See [Project Commitments](/partners/commitments) for the detailed obligations.

This allows the allocation to be delivered without requiring each seller to settle individually. The arrangement must be established before the market opens.

<Note>
Moving points into escrow does not complete settlement. The associated allocation must still become available and reach the settlement contracts.
</Note>

### Ongoing redemption

Some points programs support redemption for project-defined benefits without a planned token launch or allocation. These markets may operate without an allocation-based settlement event or scheduled closing date. Where no such settlement is required, its absence is not a settlement default.

Redemption requires read and write access to the project’s points system. Checkpoint works with the project to develop the redemption flow.

See [Project Commitments](/partners/commitments#ongoing-redemption) for the applicable redemption commitments.

## Claim

Eligible projects can claim their fee share under the [release conditions and schedule](/partners/fees-and-revenue#claiming-project-fees). Receiving fees requires a signed [standard agreement](/partners/commitments).

The fee split is fixed on market open and cannot be changed afterward.

<Info>
For allocation-based markets, users claim distributions according to their escrow-token holdings once distribution is finalized. See [Program Settlement](/architecture/program-settlement) for the claim process.
</Info>
