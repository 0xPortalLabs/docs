A project's share covers trading and cancellation fees collected from its points markets, paid in the market's payment token.

## Fee split

The standard project share is 30%. Oracle escrow settlement and Ongoing redemption must be arranged before market opening to qualify for 50%. Compare [integration requirements](/partners/settlement).

| Market model | Project | Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller settlement | 30% | 70% |
| Oracle escrow settlement | 50% | 50% |
| Ongoing redemption | 50% | 50% |

<Warning>
The fee split is fixed on market open and cannot be changed afterward.
</Warning>

## Included fees

### Trading fee

Charged on the filled trade amount in the market's USD-denominated stablecoin and deducted from seller proceeds. Called `settlementFee` in the contract, it is collected once:

- **Seller settlement:** when the offer successfully settles.
- **Oracle escrow settlement and Ongoing redemption:** when each trade is filled.

### Cancellation fee

Charged when cancelling an offer with no active fills:

- **With collateral:** calculated on locked collateral and deducted from the seller's refund.
- **Without collateral:** calculated on the listed stablecoin price and collected from the seller's wallet, requiring sufficient balance and approval.

The project share applies to the combined stablecoin fees collected from both sources.

## Excluded fees

The following remain with Checkpoint:

- **Mint fee:** additional escrow tokens minted on each fill to fund AMM pools. Calculated on the buyer's actual escrow mint; it increases supply without deducting the payment token or escrow tokens from the buyer.
- **Deposit fees:** excluded if enabled in future.
- **Other fees:** API and Pro subscriptions, fees from other projects’ markets and any fees not explicitly included in the project’s fee share.

AMM trading fees support liquidity providers and potentially Checkpoint. They are currently excluded from the project fee split.

## Claiming project fees

Receiving project fees requires a signed [fee-sharing agreement](/partners/commitments). The claim deadline is 90 days after settlement is finalized or the market is closed, whichever occurs first.

Projects using Seller settlement can sign after market opening and claim the standard 30% share before that deadline, including eligible fees collected before signing.

### Release conditions

For Seller settlement and Oracle escrow settlement, project fees remain withheld until settlement is finalized and the project has made the required allocation available to sellers or the designated oracle address, respectively.

For Oracle escrow settlement, the fee-sharing agreement may permit early release of up to 20 percentage points of eligible market fees from the project’s 50% share. At least 30 percentage points remain withheld until those conditions are met.

The agreement must specify approval and verification requirements, payment schedules and [repayment conditions](/partners/commitments#fee-release).

### Ongoing redemption

Redemption requires read and write access to the project’s points system. Eligible project fees are immediately claimable as collected under the signed fee-sharing agreement, provided Checkpoint retains the read and write access needed for users to redeem points. The 90-day claim deadline starts when the market is closed.

### Unclaimed fees

Unclaimed project fees expire 90 days after settlement is finalized or the market is closed, whichever occurs first. At that deadline, they become payable to the Checkpoint treasury. The transfer requires a transaction.

An unsigned agreement or missing receiving wallet does not extend the deadline.

Market closure does not make withheld project fees claimable. The applicable delivery commitments must still be fulfilled.

Their use follows Checkpoint’s treasury governance, including DAO governance once active. These funds may be used for governance-approved spending, including payments under ongoing DAO-approved commitments.
