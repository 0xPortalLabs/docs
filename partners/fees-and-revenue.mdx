A project's share covers trading and cancellation fees collected from its points markets, paid in the market's payment token.

## Fee split

The standard project share is 30%. Oracle escrow settlement must be arranged before market opening to qualify for 50%. Compare [settlement types](/partners/settlement).

| Settlement type | Project | Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller settlement | 30% | 70% |
| Oracle escrow settlement | 50% | 50% |

<Warning>
The fee split is fixed on market open and cannot be changed afterward.
</Warning>

## Included fees

### Trading fee

Charged on the filled trade amount in the market's USD-denominated stablecoin and deducted from seller proceeds. Called `settlementFee` in the contract, it is collected once:

- **Seller settlement:** when the offer successfully settles.
- **Oracle escrow settlement:** when each trade is filled.

### Cancellation fee

Charged when cancelling an offer with no active fills:

- **With collateral:** calculated on locked collateral and deducted from the seller's refund.
- **Without collateral:** calculated on the listed USDC price and collected from the seller's wallet, requiring sufficient balance and approval.

The project share applies to the combined USDC fees collected from both sources.

## Excluded fees

The following remain with Checkpoint:

- **`fillExtra`:** additional escrow tokens minted on each fill to fund AMM pools. Calculated on the buyer's actual escrow mint; it increases supply without deducting USDC or tokens from the buyer.
- **Deposit fees:** excluded if enabled in future.
- **Other fees:** API and Pro subscriptions, fees from other projects’ markets and any fees not explicitly included in the project’s fee share.

AMM trading fees support liquidity providers and potentially Checkpoint. They are currently excluded from the project fee split.

## Claiming project fees

Eligible projects can claim after markets settle, under the applicable conditions and schedule. Receiving fees requires a signed [standard agreement](/partners/commitments) and fulfillment of the applicable TGE and allocation-delivery commitments.

Projects can sign after market opening for the default fee share. Any unclaimed share goes to the Checkpoint treasury after settlement.
