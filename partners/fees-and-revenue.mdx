A project's share covers trading and cancellation fees collected from its points markets, paid in the market's payment token.

## Fee split

The standard project share is 30%. Oracle escrow settlement must be arranged before market opening to qualify for 50%. Compare [settlement types](/partners/settlement).

| Settlement type | Project | Checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller settlement | 30% | 70% |
| Oracle escrow settlement | 50% | 50% |

<Warning>
The fee split is fixed on market open and cannot be changed afterward.
</Warning>

## Included fees

### Trading fee

Charged on the filled trade amount in the market's USD-denominated stablecoin and deducted from seller proceeds. Called `settlementFee` in the contract, it is collected once:

- **Seller settlement:** when the offer successfully settles.
- **Oracle escrow settlement:** when each trade is filled.

### Cancellation fee

Charged when cancelling an offer with no active fills:

- **With collateral:** calculated on locked collateral and deducted from the seller's refund.
- **Without collateral:** calculated on the listed stablecoin price and collected from the seller's wallet, requiring sufficient balance and approval.

The project share applies to the combined stablecoin fees collected from both sources.

## Excluded fees

The following remain with Checkpoint:

- **Mint fee:** additional escrow tokens minted on each fill to fund AMM pools. Calculated on the buyer's actual escrow mint; it increases supply without deducting the payment token or escrow tokens from the buyer.
- **Deposit fees:** excluded if enabled in future.
- **Other fees:** API and Pro subscriptions, fees from other projects’ markets and any fees not explicitly included in the project’s fee share.

AMM trading fees support liquidity providers and potentially Checkpoint. They are currently excluded from the project fee split.

## Claiming project fees

Receiving project fees requires a signed [standard agreement](/partners/commitments). To receive the standard 30% share of eligible market fees, projects must sign and claim no later than one month after successful settlement. Eligible fees include those collected before signing.

### Release conditions

Project fees are held until successful settlement and fulfillment of the applicable TGE and allocation-delivery commitments. Ending trading does not make these fees claimable. If settlement never completes, these fees remain withheld from the project.

An oracle escrow agreement may permit early release of up to 20 percentage points of eligible market fees, out of the project's 50% share. Early release is not automatic. The remaining project share, at least 30 percentage points, stays withheld until successful settlement and fulfillment of the applicable commitments. Early release is subject to the [repayment conditions](/partners/commitments#early-fee-release) in the agreement.

### Unclaimed fees

For both settlement models, any project fees unclaimed one month after successful settlement are transferred to the Checkpoint treasury. Once transferred, they are no longer claimable by the project.

Their use follows Checkpoint’s treasury governance, including DAO governance once active. These funds may be used for governance-approved spending, including payments under ongoing DAO-approved commitments.
