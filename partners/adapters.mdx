An adapter retrieves points data from a project's endpoint and returns it in a format the Checkpoint Aggregator can use. Once the adapter proves reliable, the next stage is opening a market.

## Add your points program

[Submit an adapter PR](https://github.com/0xPortalLabs/points-adapters) for the Checkpoint Aggregator. Include your project details and reach out to Checkpoint core contributors.

The repository's [getting started guide](https://github.com/0xPortalLabs/points-adapters#getting-started) covers adapter implementation and contribution requirements.

## Adapter requirements

The adapter must:

- Return accurate points balances for the correct account and program.
- Distinguish seasons or balances with different allocation rules.
- Report endpoint errors and unavailable data without returning them as valid balances.
- Remain compatible with changes to the project's points system.

The underlying endpoint must remain reliable so Checkpoint can consistently verify points balances.

<Warning>
Frequent adapter failures can result in markets being paused until reliable points verification is restored.
</Warning>

See the [Points API](/core-products/point-api) and [Oracle System](/architecture/oracle-system) for how adapter data is used.

## Points program considerations

Predictable points issuance helps market participants assess dilution and compare balances. A defined supply cap or issuance schedule, clear season boundaries and visibility into total points issued can support this.

Where points determine a future allocation, clarity on how additional issuance and program changes affect eligibility is also useful.

## From aggregator to market

Adding an adapter to the aggregator does not automatically open a market. Once it proves reliable, Checkpoint core contributors can progress to market configuration and opening.

Project participation is optional. An adapter does not itself establish eligibility to receive fees; the requirements are covered under [Project Commitments](/partners/commitments).
