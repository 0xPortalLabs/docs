## In a nutshell

**Checkpoint provides markets for trading points, with a share of eligible market smart contract fees reserved for the project issuing those points.**

To get started, [submit an adapter PR](https://github.com/0xPortalLabs/points-adapters) for the Checkpoint Aggregator. Once the adapter proves reliable, the next stage is opening a market.

Project participation is optional. Checkpoint can open a market independently and reserve the standard project fee share.

- **Read-only access:** sellers deliver their allocations. **30% of eligible market smart contract fees is reserved for the project**.
- **Read and write access:** arranged before the market opens. The project supports points escrow and allocation delivery and receives **50% of eligible market smart contract fees**.

<Warning>
The fee split is fixed on market open and cannot be changed afterward.
</Warning>

Claiming the project fee share requires a signed standard agreement and fulfillment of the market's TGE and allocation-delivery commitments. Any unclaimed share goes to the Checkpoint treasury after settlement.

## Integration flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your points program">
    [Submit an adapter PR](https://github.com/0xPortalLabs/points-adapters) for the Checkpoint Aggregator. Include your project details and reach out to Checkpoint core contributors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose settlement type">
    Decide between seller settlement or oracle escrow settlement. Compare the [two settlement models](/partners/settlement).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply market and oracle parameters">
    Provide the market and oracle parameters required to open the market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the standard agreement">
    Sign Checkpoint’s [standard agreement](/partners/commitments) to receive fees. Projects can sign after market opening for the default fee share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement and fee distribution">
    Once settlement is available and markets have settled, eligible projects can claim their fee share under the applicable conditions and schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>
