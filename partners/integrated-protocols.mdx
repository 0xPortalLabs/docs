## In a nutshell

**Checkpoint provides markets for trading points, with a share of eligible fees collected by the market smart contract reserved for the project issuing those points.**

Checkpoint can open a market without project participation and reserve the standard 30% project fee share. The market’s registered custody mode determines the split:

- **No custody:** sellers deliver their allocations and **30% of eligible market smart contract fees is reserved for the project**.
- **Registered custody:** arranged before a market opens. The project supports the applicable points transfers and allocation or redemption commitments. **50% of eligible market smart contract fees is reserved for the project**.

<Warning>
The fee split is fixed on market open and cannot be changed afterward.
</Warning>

Claiming the project fee share requires a signed fee-sharing agreement.

To get started, [submit an adapter PR](https://github.com/0xPortalLabs/points-adapters) for the Checkpoint Aggregator. Once the adapter proves reliable, the next stage is opening a market.

## Integration flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your points program">
    [Submit an adapter PR](https://github.com/0xPortalLabs/points-adapters) for the Checkpoint Aggregator. Include your project details and reach out to Checkpoint core contributors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose market model">
    Compare Seller settlement, Oracle escrow settlement and Ongoing redemption in the [integration requirements](/partners/settlement).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply market and oracle parameters">
    Provide the market and oracle parameters required to open the market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the fee-sharing agreement">
    Sign Checkpoint’s [fee-sharing agreement](/partners/commitments) to receive fees. Projects can sign after market opening for the default fee share, subject to the claim deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery and fee distribution">
    Eligible projects can claim their fee share under the applicable [release conditions and schedule](/partners/fees-and-revenue#claiming-project-fees).
  </Step>
</Steps>
