## Written agreement

Under this proposal, participating-protocol status and fee sharing require a written agreement under either settlement model. That agreement should identify the parties and their authority, the covered programs and seasons, and their responsibilities for:

- Maintaining data access and notifying Checkpoint of material changes.
- Recognizing sold or escrowed points under the agreed allocation rules.
- Handling partial sales, bonuses, multipliers, eligibility requirements, and allocation caps.
- Delivering or enabling claims for the allocation under the selected settlement model.
- Calculating, verifying, and paying the fee share.
- Handling interruptions, disputes, delays, and failure to deliver.

## Allocation commitments

The agreement and market disclosures must distinguish any committed allocation from a discretionary reward. An integration must not imply a guaranteed token launch, allocation, or value where none has been committed.

If no allocation occurs, the response depends on those commitments and the disclosed market rules. The agreement must define the applicable process, including any available user recovery measures, before trading begins.

## Policy changes and authority

Terms should identify the policy version that applies to each program. Changes for future integrations should follow a documented approval process; changes affecting existing obligations should follow the applicable agreement. The authority of the foundation, operating entity, and any future DAO remains to be specified.

## Escrow configuration to confirm

For oracle escrow settlement, confirm the designated oracle address, the points-transfer method and how transfers are recorded, including partial sales and adjustments. Define how escrowed points retain allocation eligibility and how the resulting allocation reaches the settlement contracts.

These details need technical review before they become agreement requirements.

## Decisions to finalize

- The process for delays, changed program rules, or no allocation.
- Available user recovery measures and how disputes are handled.
- Which entity signs and administers agreements, and who approves future policy changes.

The agreement should reference the selected [settlement model](/partners/settlement) and [fee terms](/partners/fees-and-revenue).
