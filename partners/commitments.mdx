## Fee-sharing agreement

Receiving project fees requires signing the fee-sharing agreement with Checkpoint Exchange. The commitments below apply to projects under their signed fee-sharing agreement.

The agreement identifies the parties and their authority, the covered points programs, seasons and markets and their responsibilities for:

- Maintaining the required data access and promptly notifying Checkpoint of material changes or interruptions that may affect settlement or redemption.
- Defining and honoring allocation or redemption commitments attached to sold points, distinguishing optional rewards from committed benefits and ensuring subsequent program changes do not undermine settlement or redemption.
- Receiving project fees under the release conditions applicable to the market model.
- Defining how access withdrawal, program discontinuation or market termination affects outstanding delivery commitments and accrued fees, including withholding and the transfer of unclaimed fees to treasury after the applicable claim deadline.

The same core commitments apply to all projects signing the fee-sharing agreement.

For Seller settlement, if a market has already opened, a project can still sign the fee-sharing agreement to claim the default 30% share of eligible fees before the [claim deadline](/partners/fees-and-revenue#claiming-project-fees).

## Delivery commitments

The project’s delivery commitments depend on the market model and are defined in the fee-sharing agreement.

### Seller settlement

- Sold points remain in sellers’ accounts and sellers are responsible for delivering the allocation for their filled offers.
- The project must make the allocation attributable to sold points available to sellers so they can settle their filled offers.

### Oracle escrow settlement

The project provides Checkpoint with read and write access to its points program so sold points can be removed from sellers’ accounts and credited to the designated oracle address.

- The project must preserve allocation eligibility for escrowed points and deliver the resulting allocation to the oracle address for distribution to buyers.
- The agreement records the designated oracle address, the points-transfer method and how transfers are recorded, including partial sales and balance adjustments.
- This integration must be arranged before the market opens.

### Ongoing redemption

The project provides Checkpoint with read and write access to its points system so users can redeem points themselves.

- The agreement defines the redemption benefits, eligibility, limits and conditions.
- Ongoing redemption may operate without a token launch, final settlement event or scheduled market closure.
- The absence of an allocation or settlement event is not a failure to deliver where neither is required.

## Fee release

- **Oracle escrow settlement:** the fee-sharing agreement may permit early release under the applicable [release conditions](/partners/fees-and-revenue#release-conditions).
- **Ongoing redemption:** eligible fees are immediately claimable as collected, provided Checkpoint retains the read and write access needed for users to redeem points.
- If the project fails to fulfill its commitments, Checkpoint may withhold unpaid project fees and/or seek repayment of fees already released under the fee-sharing agreement.

## Points-trading exclusivity

Separate trading systems can allow the same points to be sold more than once, creating competing claims to the same allocation or redemption benefits. Exclusivity helps reduce this risk by keeping project-supported trading within Checkpoint infrastructure.

- For the covered points programs, the project must use Checkpoint as its exclusive official points-trading integration during the exclusivity period.
- The project must not integrate with, provide trading-specific support to or promote competing points-trading venues for those programs.
- Under the applicable agreement, projects and apps can integrate Checkpoint’s trading infrastructure into their own sites.
- Independent third-party listings do not by themselves constitute a project breach.

The fee-sharing agreement must define the covered programs, exclusivity period and treatment of existing trading arrangements.

## Policy changes and authority

The agreement identifies the policy version that applies to each covered program.

- Changes for future integrations follow a documented approval process.
- Changes affecting existing obligations must follow the applicable agreement.
- The fee split remains fixed at market opening.

The agreement references the applicable versions of the [settlement or redemption requirements](/partners/settlement) and [fee terms](/partners/fees-and-revenue).
