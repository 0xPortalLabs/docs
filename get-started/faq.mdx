## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Checkpoint?" icon="circle-question">
    Checkpoint is a protocol that lets you check, track and trade your points across any project.

    Projects reward your activity with points that eventually convert into tokens at a Token
    Generation Event (TGE). Until now, those points just sat there with no way to buy, sell or do
    anything with them. Checkpoint changes that by giving points a market.

    In one place you can:

    - View your points across 70+ integrated protocols
    - Deposit your points to make them tradeable
    - Buy points from other users or sell yours at a price you set
    - Track your full points history over time

    Your effort has value. Checkpoint gives it a market.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which chain is it running on?" icon="link">
    Checkpoint connects points from 70+ protocols across various chains.

    The testnet runs on Arbitrum Sepolia, but you won't feel it. Gas is fully abstracted and USDC is
    mintless. The experience is seamless, with no bridging or gas management needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I join the testnet?" icon="door-open">
    Open the web app and start trading, that's it. No waitlist, no invite code. Connect your wallet
    and you're in.

    [checkpoint.exchange](https://checkpoint.exchange)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I connect a wallet?" icon="wallet">
    1. Go to [checkpoint.exchange](https://checkpoint.exchange)
    2. Tap **Connect Wallet** button
    3. Sign the verification, one tap, no gas
    4. Done. Your wallet is connected
      (+10 XP earned automatically on first connection.)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I earn Checkpoint XP?" icon="sparkles">
    XP is how Checkpoint tracks your participation during testnet. Here's how to earn:

    - Connect your wallet -> **+10 XP** one time
    - Deposit your points -> **+100 XP** one time
    - Trade points -> **+5 XP** per trade, max 5x per day

    The more you explore, the more XP you stack.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I deposit my points?" icon="inbox">
    You deposit points the first time you sell. Here's how:

    1. Tap **Markets** from the left menu
    2. You'll see current open markets
    3. Select a market where you have points, for example **Checkpoint**
    4. You're now on the trading screen
    5. Set up your sell order, including how many points and at what price, click **Deposit Points** button when prompted
    6. Complete the wallet verification
    7. Done. Your points are deposited and ready to trade
       (+100 XP earned on your first deposit.)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sell my points?" icon="store">
    1. Tap **Markets** from the left menu
    2. You'll see current open markets
    3. Select a market, for example **Checkpoint**
    4. You're now on the trading screen
    5. Set your price and amount, choose how many points and at what price
    6. You don't need to sell everything at once, partial orders are supported
    7. **First time selling?** You'll need to deposit your points first. Just click the button and
       complete the wallet verification, then place your sell order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I buy points?" icon="shopping-cart">
    1. Open **Markets** from the left menu
    2. You'll see current open markets
    3. Select a market, for example **Checkpoint**
    4. You're now on the trading screen
    5. Tap any order that looks good to you from the orderbook
    6. Hit **Fill Buy Order** to complete
    7. No manual signing needed, smart wallet handles everything
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I buy or sell partially?" icon="split">
    Yes. Checkpoint allows partial fills on both sides, you don't have to buy or sell an exact
    amount. When creating an offer, you can set any amount up to 6 decimal places. The most common
    sizes used by community are 0.01 and 0.1.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I redeposit my points?" icon="arrows-rotate">
    There's no manual redeposit process. When you deposit for the first time, a unique NFT is minted
    to your wallet, that's your permanent points receipt.

    If you've sold all your previously deposited points and have earned more since, you don't need
    to do anything. Your points balance will be updated onchain automatically by the Oracle. Just
    keep in mind there's a 24-48 hour delay before the update reflects onchain.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my offer?" icon="ban">
    Yes, as long as no one has partially filled it yet. Once an offer has been partially filled, it
    can no longer be cancelled. Here's how:

    - Open the market where you created the offer
    - Go to **My Offers** tab
    - Tap **Active Offers**
    - Hit **Cancel** button on the left
  </Accordion>
