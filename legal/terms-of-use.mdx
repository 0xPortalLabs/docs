Where no allocation-based settlement is required, the absence of such an event does not constitute a settlement default.

For markets requiring allocation delivery, the settlement type affects when sellers receive payment and how allocations are delivered:

Where sold points are transferred into escrow within the project’s points system, that transfer does not itself deliver or fund a future token allocation.

Where allocation-based settlement applies, receiving escrow tokens or completing a trade does not mean that allocation delivery or the program’s final distribution has occurred.
