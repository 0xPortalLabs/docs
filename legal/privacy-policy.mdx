**Last Updated: November 2026**

Checkpoint Exchange Corp., a company incorporated in Panama (the “Company”), is responsible for the processing described in this Privacy Policy in connection with the Services.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Company collects, uses and shares personal information in connection with `checkpoint.exchange` (the “Site”), `app.checkpoint.exchange`, `docs.checkpoint.exchange` and other Checkpoint interfaces (collectively, the “Interface”), together with other online locations that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Services”). References to “Checkpoint” in this Privacy Policy mean the Company unless the context expressly refers to the Checkpoint Protocol (the “Protocol”).

The Company provides interfaces and supporting infrastructure for the Protocol. It may process off-chain information and information obtained from public blockchain networks. Public blockchain records are generally immutable and are not under the Company’s unilateral control to alter or remove, although smart contracts and oracle mechanisms may reflect updated information over time.

This Privacy Policy describes the Company’s data practices. Where applicable law requires consent for particular processing, that consent will be requested separately. Use of the Services remains subject to the Terms of Use. Changes to this Privacy Policy are described in section 12.

## 1. Information Collection

The Company may collect your name, contact information and message content when you communicate with the Company, such as providing feedback or asking for support. If you voluntarily share additional information with the Company, you are solely responsible for it.

Wallet authentication and smart-account features may involve connected and embedded wallet addresses, smart-account addresses, signed authentication messages and authorizations, account or session identifiers and related technical records. The Company may process this information to provide wallet connections, authentication and transaction features and for security, fraud prevention and access restrictions.

The Company and providers supporting the Services may also process IP addresses, browser and device information, usage information and diagnostic records to operate, secure, troubleshoot, analyze and improve the Services.

When you create or interact with offers, trades, escrow contracts, collateral deposits, escrow tokens, settlement contracts, oracle claims or other protocol actions through the Interface, the associated transaction details (including involved addresses, market identifiers, offer identifiers, token or point amounts, collateral amounts, prices, fees, timestamps, settlement status, claim status and other on-chain parameters) are recorded on public blockchain networks and may be publicly viewable and linkable to your wallet address. The Company may also collect, generate, index, display or process related off-chain metadata where needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, analyze or improve exchange, escrow, settlement, oracle and market features.

In providing the Services, the Company may retrieve and display wallet-linked points, balances, eligibility and activity information from project endpoints, adapters, public blockchain networks and other data providers.

The Services may also allow you to collect, view, accrue, claim or otherwise interact with Checkpoint-created points, scores, badges, campaign records or similar non-transferable metrics (“Checkpoint Points”). In connection with Checkpoint Points, the Company may collect, generate, receive or display information such as wallet addresses, campaign participation, referral or invite data, eligibility criteria, point calculations, timestamps, endpoint responses, API requests, device or session information and related anti-fraud, abuse-prevention, analytics and support records. Some Checkpoint Points information may be publicly displayed through leaderboards, profiles, campaign pages, APIs, endpoints or other Service features and may be linkable to your wallet address or other identifiers you provide.

For clarity, Checkpoint Points records are data records only. They do not represent cash, monetary value, property, digital assets, tokens, stored value, rewards currency or any right to redemption, exchange, conversion, allocation, distribution, payment or other benefit. Any future treatment, recognition or use of Checkpoint Points, if any, is subject to the Terms of Use and may be determined, changed, suspended or declined by Checkpoint, the Protocol, any DAO, any governance process or any current or future team or contributor.

If you choose to connect accounts, submit identifiers, use referral links, participate in campaigns or interact with public or semi-public features, you understand that related information may be visible to other users or third parties depending on the feature. Please do not submit information that you do not want associated with your wallet address, profile, campaign activity or public Service activity.

If you use enhanced, paid, invite-only, promotional or limited-access features such as Checkpoint Pro, Checkpoint Premium or similar plans, the Company may collect and process information needed to provide and manage those features. This may include subscription or plan status, eligibility, usage limits, tracked wallet addresses, wallet lists, labels, preferences, alerts, historical data queries, exports, support requests, billing-related metadata, payment status, renewal or cancellation status and fraud-prevention or abuse-prevention records. Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers and the Company may receive limited payment or billing metadata from them; unless expressly stated otherwise, the Company does not collect or store full payment card details directly.

## 5. Cookies and Browser Storage

The Company and providers supporting the Services may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for authentication, session management, security and saved preferences. These technologies may contain wallet, account or session identifiers and can involve personal information.

**How are these technologies used?**

- **Authentication and security**: Supporting wallet connections, maintaining sessions and protecting the Services.
- **Preferences**: Remembering settings such as theme or wallet connection preferences.
- **Analytics and diagnostics**: Understanding usage and investigating errors. These functions do not necessarily use cookies; Fathom provides cookieless analytics.

**Managing browser storage**

Most browsers provide controls for cookies and site data, including local storage. Consult your browser’s help documentation for guidance on restricting or deleting these records. Disabling certain technologies may affect the functionality or performance of the Services.

Where applicable law requires consent for particular technologies, the Company will request that consent separately.
